I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Technology Systems at East Carolina University. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln in 2020. Prior to joining ECU, I was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Texas Permian Basin (2020–2022).
My research interests include:
* misbehavior detection,
* cyber-physical systems,
* machine learning,
* intelligent transportation systems, 
* the application of large language models in cyber defense and response.
I am an active member of IEEE and the IEEE Communications Society. In addition, I serve as a reviewer and technical program committee member for several leading journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing, IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security, IEEE Wireless Communications Magazine, IEEE Communications Magazine, IEEE Network Magazine, as well as flagship conferences such as INFOCOM, GLOBECOM, ICC, and VTC.

## Experience
* Assistant Professor, August 2022 - present, Department of Technology Systems, East Carolina University, Greenville, NC, USA.
* Assistant Professor, August 2020 - August 2022, Department of Computer Science, The University of Texas Permian Basin, Odessa, TX, USA.
